# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about code, not about each other.

## Expected

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith
- Critique the change, not the author — "this leaks a file descriptor" not "you don't understand Go"
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change; scope is a judgement call, not a personal slight

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexual attention, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other project space, plus public
spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to tranchihuu12.03@gmail.com. Reports stay private. Maintainers will
respond with whatever the situation calls for — a warning, deleting a comment, or a
ban — and are expected to hold themselves to the same standard.
